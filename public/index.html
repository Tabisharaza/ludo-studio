OUR TABLE, OUR RULES

The house rules.

  1. Six leaves the yard. Use a 6 to bring a piece from the yard to its colored start square, or move an eligible piece six steps.
  2. Every roll must be used. An unusable roll undoes moves since your most recent capture. Before any capture, it undoes the entire turn. Every capture permanently commits the board: captured pieces stay in the yard, and earlier moves stay done. If your last piece needs 3, an unusable 6 ends the turn immediately. You cannot skip it and roll a 3.
  3. Capture, then switch pieces. Land on an opponent to send their pieces to the yard. The capturing piece is locked for the rest of this turn. You earn another roll, but must use a different piece. A 6 can bring a remaining yard piece out.
  4. Finish exactly. Travel clockwise, then along your colored home lane. You must roll the exact number to finish. The exact finish of your fourth piece ends the game immediately, with all rolled values used and no further bonus roll owed.
  5. Bonus rolls and safe spots. Sixes and captures earn one bonus roll, even when both happen together. Three consecutive sixes undo moves since the most recent capture, or the whole turn if there was no capture. Finishing a non-final piece does not earn a bonus. Ring-marked squares and colored starts are safe. Pieces may share and pass squares; no blockades.

House rules vary. These rules are the ones used at this table. Play locally on one screen or online on separate devices with 2–4 people. Online rolls and moves are checked by the server. Press Space to roll or 1–4 to choose a legal piece; buttons also work with Tab and Enter.

Privacy: no accounts, chat, ads, analytics or purchases. Online tables store game state and temporary, hashed seat sessions for up to 24 hours; expired records are removed when a new table is created. Local progress stays on this device. Hosting infrastructure may process ordinary network logs.
